### Introduction
My name is Peyton Nguyen. I am currently a Senior Software Engineer at Tesla. I have been working at Tesla as a full stack developer for 2 years now. I work on an internal suite of tools that focus on reducing the cost of a Tesla vehicle. Our applications are heavily involved in the entire lifetime of a part. We help our stakeholders, who are Supply Chain Managers, identify opportunities for cost savings. We help them both procure parts at a cheaper price and also help simulation many different models and workflows that a particular commodity goes through to identify these opportunities. We also have developed a very highly impactful executive dashboard that is used by SVPs and VPs. A week ago, it was actually also used by Elon. This dashboard shows the progress of different opportunities, showing what is in the Ideation phase up until the Implementation phase. Annually, our team saves Tesla around 150 million dollars and this is just the opportunities that we know about that happen online. Not considering what happens offline through emails with Suppliers and Supply Chain Managers.
